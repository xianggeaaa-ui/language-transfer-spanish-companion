# Lesson 01 — Introduction

Lesson 1 is the orientation lesson — Mihalis explains the method (no new grammar/vocab to memorise yet). Key Points begin from Lesson 2.

# Lesson 02 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • Words ending in -al: English→Spanish only change the stress (normal→normal, metal→metal, legal→legal, ilegal, ideal, natural, total, personal…). Vowels are read as spelled; stress falls on the last syllable.
  • `es` = is / he (she, it, you-formal) is.
  • `no` = not; `no es` = is not.
  • Spanish is a phonetic language — read it as spelled.

# Lesson 03 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • `-ant / -ent → add -e`: important→importante, diferente, constante (stress on the second-to-last syllable).
  • `-mente = -ly (adverb)`: constantemente, legalmente, normalmente, generalmente, probablemente…
  • The /h/ sound → /x/ (velar fricative): global→global, general, digital, original.
  • `-ible / -able → Spanish forms`: posible, probable, flexible (stress on second-to-last syllable).
  • `normalmente` = usually.

# Lesson 04 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • `-ion rule`: English words ending in -ion keep the same form in Spanish, stress on the last syllable (opinion→opinión, passion→pasión).
  • `-tion → -ción`: English -tion becomes -ción in Spanish, pronounced /s/ in most regions (preparación).
  • Noun minus -ción plus -r gives the verb: preparación→preparar, generación→generar, confirmación→confirmar, complicación→complicar, cooperación→cooperar, cancelación→cancelar, exploración→explorar.
  • Word breakdown: confirmación = con (with) + firma (signature); confrontación comes from frente (forehead).
  • Negation: place no directly before the verb (No quiero).
  • te quiero (I want you / I love you, everyday) vs te amo (more solemn).
  • Reflexive: preparar + me → prepararme (to get oneself ready).

# Lesson 05 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • yo (I): used only for emphasis (Yo quiero).
  • `-ence / -ance → -encia / -ancia` rule: difference→diferencia, influence→influencia, existence→existencia, consistence→consistencia, incongruence→incongruencia.
  • lo = it / him, also formal you.
  • pero (but); perro (dog, used to distinguish single r from double rr trill).
  • Reflexive pronouns: informarme, obligarte, provocarte, salvarlo, etc. (me/te/lo attach after the verb).
  • Noun → verb: obligación→obligar, provocar (from provoke), salvación→salvar, situación→situar, participación→participar, creación→crear, experimentar (cognate with experiencia/experimento).

# Lesson 06 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • `-o ending marks 'I'`: intento = I try / I am trying.
  • h is silent: hospital, horrible actually start with a vowel sound (imagine they begin with o).
  • ahora (now) = a (toward) + hora (hour); hora = hour.
  • voy = I go (cognate with voyage), v often sounds like soft b; vamos = we go / let's go.
  • `-mente adverb`: habitual → habitualmente (adjective + mente).
  • From visitation → visitar; visible / viral / virus are identical; conveniente = convenient.

# Lesson 07 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • voy a + infinitive — near future / verb of motion (e.g. Voy a continuar)
  • Quiero + infinitive — 'I want to' (e.g. Quiero mostrarte algo)
  • lo / la / los / las — direct-object pronoun (e.g. verlos = see them)
  • intentar — 'to try' (intento = I try)
  • mostrar — 'to show' (mostrarte = show you)
  • pasar — 'to pass / pass by' (voy a pasar a visitar = I'm going to pass by to visit)

# Lesson 08 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **a = to**; a + come/go verbs (venir a).

# Lesson 11 — Key Points & What It Covers

# Lesson 12 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **-ar verbs**: drop -ar, add -o(I) / -a(he/she/you) / -an(they/you) / -as(you). Key vowel /a/. e.g. contaminar to contamina/contaminan/contaminas; organizar to organiza/organizan/organizas.
  • **-er/-ir verbs**: key vowel /e/, add -o/-e/-en/-es. e.g. vender to vendo/vende/venden.
  • **Direct object pronouns**: lo (it/them, masc), los (them, masc), la (it, fem / fem definite article), las (them, fem / fem definite article). Can attach after infinitive or go before verb.
  • **Feminine endings**: -a (la casa), -ion (la administracion), -idad (la diversidad). English -ity to -idad, -tion to -ion, -ary to -ario.
  • **claro can also mean 'of course'**.

# Lesson 14 — Key Points & What It Covers

# Lesson 15 — Key Points & What It Covers

# Lesson 16 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **In present, stem-stressed e to ie, o to ue diphthong (stem-changing)**; when stress on second-to-last syllable e/o easily diphthongize.
  • **A verb form can serve as a noun**: add un/el to make it a noun (un encuentro = a meeting, una muestra = a sample).
  • **el = masc definite article / he**; la = fem definite article (this lesson shows la muerte).
  • **z in most of Spain reads /th/ (empezar)**.

# Lesson 17 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **e to ie, o to ue diphthong only in stressed position**; we form (-amos/-emos/-imos) doesn't stress stem so no diphthong.
  • **Arabic-source words often start with al-**: alcohol, azucar, arroz, alfombra, almohada, aceite, aceituna.
  • **Spanish doesn't use two s in a row** (posible/pasion single s); two r distinguish trill (arroz).
  • **New we form**: -amos (hablamos, tomamos, pensamos, cerramos).
  • **y = and (conjunction)**.

# Lesson 18 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **we form**: -ar to -amos, -er to -emos, -ir to -imos; this is the only place -er/-ir differ.
  • **Spanish v actually sounds like soft b** (vivir, verano); places aqui/acA = here; donde = where; siempre = always.
  • **Vowel-harmony hint**: vive is not veve.

# Lesson 19 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Feminine endings**: -ion, -idad (la opinion, la oportunidad); words ending in e need gender checked (carne feminine).

# Lesson 20 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **nos = us (object/reflexive)**; nos vemos = see you (we see each other).

# Lesson 25 — Key Points & What It Covers

# Lesson 29 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **siempre = always (flexible position)**.
  • **muerto/muerta = dead (in Spanish a state, use estar)**.

# Lesson 31 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **e- prothesis rule (s + consonant)**: English words beginning with s+consonant (st/sp/s+cons) get an added e- in Spanish. state to estar; Spanish to espanol; Spain to Espana; student to estudiante; school to escuela; special to especial; spatial to espacial; strict to estricto; spectacular to espectacular.
  • **ser vs estar (essence/characteristic vs state)**: ser expresses essence/characteristic (ser itself = to exist/to be; un ser = a being/creature; ser humano = human being); estar expresses state.
  • **ser present tense (persons completed this lesson)**: soy (I am) / eres (you-informal are) / es (he/she/you-formal is, learned T2) / somos (we are) / son (they/you-plural are, T2). EN to ES: I am Spanish to soy espanol; you are a student to eres estudiante; we are strict to somos estrictos.
  • **Only 4 verbs have a "-oy" first-person ("I") form**: voy (ir), doy (dar), estoy (estar), soy (ser).
  • **como esta? vs como es?**: estar asks about state (how is he/she right now) to Comoo esta?; ser asks about characteristic (what kind of person) to Comoo es?

# Lesson 32 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **ser vs estar + same adjective, different meaning**: aburrido — es aburrido (boring, characteristic) / esta aburrido (bored, state).
  • **Adjective gender/number agreement (memorize masculine singular lemma; derive 4 forms by rule)**: words ending -o change with the noun's gender/number -o/-a/-os/-as (he is bored to esta aburrido; she is bored to esta aburrida; they are bored to están aburridos/aburridas; similarly ocupado/-a/-os/-as, casado/-a/-os/-as, preocupado/-a/-os/-as); words ending -e don't change gender, only add -s (libre to libres).
  • **de = from/of (glue preposition, explicitly introduced)**: appears in Latin borrowings (deliver = de + liberar "to free"; decipher = de + Arabic sifr "zero"). De donde es/eres/son? (where are you/he/they from, characteristic) vs Donde están? (where are they, state).
  • **persona safety net**: use "soy una persona + adjective" to express a characteristic, avoiding ser/estar uncertainty (I'm a busy person to soy una persona ocupada; I'm a married person to soy una persona casada); persona is feminine, adjective agrees with persona not with the speaker.
  • **Vocabulary derivation**: liberar = to free/liberate (libre = free); casado = married (from casa = home/settling down); ocupado = busy (occupied); preocupado = worried (preoccupied).

# Lesson 33 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **listo + ser/estar double meaning**: estoy listo (I'm ready, state) / soy listo (I'm clever, characteristic). Gender/number agreement same as T32: I'm ready (f.) to estoy lista; we're ready to estamos listos; they're ready (f.) to están listas.
  • **tan = so/such (degree adverb)**: I'm so tired to estoy tan cansado; it's so boring to es tan aburrido (corresponds to English overlapping -ing "so").
  • **bueno + ser/estar double meaning**: es bueno (a good person / good at, characteristic) / esta bueno (attractive, state, of a person); plural son buenos; bon appetit's o splits into bueno.
  • **feliz + ser/estar double meaning**: estoy feliz (happy right now, state) / soy feliz (a happy person, characteristic); feliz ends in a consonant, gender doesn't change. Feliz navidad to felicidad = happiness (felicity to felicidad).
  • **persona safety-net reuse**: I'm a happy person to soy una persona feliz.

# Lesson 34 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Present progressive = estar + -ando/-iendo (only for what's happening right this moment)**: -ar verbs drop -ar add -ando (hablar to hablando, preparar to preparando, esperar to esperando, desayunar to desayunando); -er/-ir verbs drop ending add -iendo (comer to comiendo, vender to vendiendo). EN to ES: I'm preparing it to lo estoy preparando; they are selling to están vendiendo; we are waiting to estamos esperando.
  • **English overuses -ing; Spanish uses it only for the present moment**: future/habitual use the present tense (I'm preparing it tomorrow to Lo preparo mañana), not estoy preparando.
  • **Pronoun position**: lo estoy preparando / estoy preparandolo (clitic can go before or attach after -ando, attaching requires adding an accent).
  • **Etymology**: desayunar = to eat breakfast (des- "undo" + ayunar "to fast"; break-fast = break the fast); ayunar = to fast; desayuno is also the noun "breakfast".

# Lesson 35 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **ser's progressive siendo**: I'm being sincere to estoy siendo sincero (ser is -er class, drop -er add -iendo); we're being honest to estamos siendo honestos.
  • **-ing form reuses T34 rule**: salir to saliendo (I'm leaving to estoy saliendo); comprar to comprando (I'm buying them to los estoy comprando / estoy comprandolos).
  • **honesto/-a (new word this lesson, use ser)**: honest to honesto; soy honesto / soy honesta; gender/number agreement same as T32 (honesto/-a/-os/-as).
  • **sincero / sinceridad are review of T12 words' ser usage**: sincere to sincero (characteristic, ser: soy sincero/a); sincerity to sinceridad.
  • **salir = to go out/leave (irregular yo: salgo not salo)**; salir itself learned T13, this lesson adds its irregular yo form and progressive.
  • **Future/habitual still uses present tense**: I'm leaving soon to salgo pronto; I'm leaving tomorrow to salgo mañana; I'm buying them next week to los compro la semana que viene; only "happening right now" uses estoy saliendo / estoy comprando.

# Lesson 36 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Future tense formation = infinitive (to-form) + person endings of haber, stress pushed to the ending**: endings -e(I) / -as(you) / -a(he/she/you) / -emos(we) / -an(they/you-plural), same sound as haber's he/has/ha/hemos/han (minus h). EN to ES: I will eat to comere; you will eat to comeras; he will eat to comera; we will eat to comeremos; they will eat to comeran.
  • **Rule applies to ALL verbs (including irregular)**: ir to ire/iras/ira/iremos/iran (ir present is irregular, future is regular); encontrar to encontrare/encontrara/encontraremos/encontraran; preparar to preparare; esperar to esperare; organizar to organizara/organizaran; ser to sere/sera; estar to estare (I will be tired to estare cansada).
  • **Spanish seldom uses the future tense**: in most cases use present or voy a + infinitive; the future exists but is rare.
  • **Pronoun/reflexive position unchanged**: I will find it to lo encontrare; he will find them to los encontrara; I will stay to me quedare (quedarse's reflexive pronoun still goes before).
  • **Vocabulary derivation**: recomendar (recommend) to recomendacion (recommendation); Iran = Iran (country name, introduced via the future-form iran "they will go" homophone).

# Lesson 37 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **dar future (applies T36 rule, regular endings)**: I will give to dare; you will give to daras; he will give to dara; we will give to daremos; they will give to daran.
  • **me doy cuenta = I realize** (lit. I give myself an account); cuenta = account; you realize to te das cuenta; we realize to nos damos cuenta. realizar = to achieve (a project), not synonymous with "realize"; mentioned only for contrast, not in the vocab list.
  • **Dative (indirect object) pronouns le / les = to/for + him/her/you-formal / them/you-plural**: third person shifts from accusative lo/la to le (singular), los/las to les (plural). EN to ES: I have spoken to him to le he hablado; they have spoken to them to les han hablado; I give them something to les doy algo.
  • **Double-object order (dative before accusative)**: they will give it to me to me lo daran; they will give it to you to te lo daran; they will give it to us to nos lo daran; he cooks them for us to nos las cocina.
  • **algo = something** (T7 learned, used here for double-object practice: les doy algo).

# Lesson 38 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Reflexive pronoun se = himself/herself/itself/themselves/each other (third-person reflexive, first introduced)**: accusative lo/la/los/las to se (expresses "self"). EN to ES: he talks to himself to se habla; they talk to each other to se hablan; he/she/you-formal stays to se queda; they stay to se quedan.
  • **Reflexive infinitive in the dictionary (se attaches to the to-form as one word)**: quedar(se) learned T26; this lesson makes explicit dar + se + cuenta to darse cuenta = to realize. EN to ES: he realizes to se da cuenta; they realize to se dan cuenta.
  • **me / te / nos already contain "self/each other" and don't change form**: alongside se they form the full reflexive paradigm me / te / se / nos / os / se (I stay to yo me quedo; you stay to tu te quedas; we stay to nosotros nos quedamos).

# Lesson 39 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Prepositions con/para/de (glue words, explicitly introduced)**: con = with (con el/ella/ellos/ellas; con nosotros/as; con usted/ustedes); para = for (para el/ella/ellos/ellas; para nosotros/as; para usted/ustedes); de = from/of (de el/ella/ellos/ellas; de ustedes). Spanish "talk to him" = con el (no accusative).
  • **After prepositions, "I/you" become mi / ti (prepositional case, not nominative)**: for me to para mi; of/from you to de ti (mi has an accent to distinguish from possessive mi=my); other persons keep nominative pronouns after prepositions (el, ella, nosotros, usted, ustedes), only 1st/2nd singular change form.
  • **ser vs estar + prepositional phrase**: it's yours to es de ustedes (characteristic) / he's with you to esta con ustedes (state, location).
  • **favor = a favor (un favor)**; hacer un favor = to do a favor (he does her a favor to le hace un favor).
  • **decir = to say/tell** (contrast hablar = to speak/talk); I want to tell him to le quiero decir / quiero decirle (clitic before or attached after the full infinitive); I want to say it to lo quiero decir.
  • **le / les replace to/for/from + person (extension of T37 rule)**: I buy for them to les compro; he buys it for me / from me to me lo compra (context disambiguates); Spanish often drops the preposition.

# Lesson 40 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **enamorarse = to fall in love (reflexive infinitive, one word)**: enamorar + se; I fall in love to me enamoro; I fall in love with you/him to me enamoro de ti / de el (Spanish uses de "of" not with).
  • **sonar = to dream (n = n+y), stem o to ue diphthong (in stressed syllable, same as T16 rule)**: I dream to sueno; you dream to suenas; he/she dreams to suena; they dream to suenan; we dream to sonamos (stress not on stem, so no diphthong). Spanish "dream of her" = sueno con ella (use con not of/about).
  • **depender = to depend (regular -er verb)**: it depends to depende; Spanish says depende de (use de not on): it depends on the price to depende del precio.
  • **conmigo = with me (one word) / contigo = with you (informal, one word)**: distinct from the two-word de mi / para mi; Do you want to come with me? to Quieres venir conmigo?; I'm going with you to voy contigo; I dream of you to sueno contigo.

# Lesson 41 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Future tense (will)**: add endings -e(I) / -as(you) / -a(he/she/it/you) / -emos(we) / -an(they/you-plural) to the infinitive, stress on the ending; works for -ar/-er/-ir. e.g. bailar to bailare / bailaras / bailara / bailaremos / bailaran.
  • **Conditional tense (would)**: add -ia(I/he/she/it/you) / -ias(you) / -iamos(we) / -ian(they/you-plural) to the infinitive, stress on ia; I and he/she/it/you share the form. e.g. bailar to bailaria / bailarias / bailariamos / bailarian.
  • **Future/conditional commonality**: both attach to the full infinitive, stress shifts to the ending, all three verb classes share one set of endings.
  • **Three ways to express future**: future tense, ir a (voy a / vamos a), or present + future-time word (mañana, mas tarde).
  • **se expresses passive / "get"**: English "it gets X-ed" to Spanish "it X-es itself". e.g. contamina to se contamina; se transformaria (would be transformed).
  • **bailar to bailarin**: to dance to dancer (ballerina is cognate).

# Lesson 51 — Key Points & What It Covers

# Lesson 52 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **para = in order to**: when English "to" carries the sense of "in order to", you must use para, the bare infinitive isn't enough: Hemos hablado para resolverlo (we've talked to resolve it); Estoy aqui para verte (I'm here to see you); Te he llamado para hablarlo (I called to talk about it).
  • **Participle formation -ado/-ido**: -ar to -ado (hablar to hablado), -er/-ir to -ido; He hablado / Hemos hablado.
  • **Object pronoun attaches to infinitive / gerund end**: resolver to resolverlo; hablar to hablarlo; ver to verte; explicar to estoy explicandolo (lo can attach to ando/iendo end or go before lo estoy explicando).
  • **Present progressive = estar + -ando(-ar) / -iendo(-er/-ir) (formation)**: estoy comiendo, estoy hablando, estoy explicando, estoy tomando.
  • **explicacion is the exception** (not *explanacion): explicar to explicacion; English "inexplicable" keeps the same /k/.
  • **-ante(-ar) / -iente(-er/-ir) = English -ing adjective**: interesar to interesante, irritar to irritante, frustrar to frustrante, menguar to menguante, salir to saliente, vivir to viviente (note not menguando).

  • **Two equivalent "I was -ing" past forms**: estaba tomando or tomaba (both mean I was taking); comia or estaba comiendo; mostly interchangeable.
  • **When -ing is the subject, Spanish also uses the infinitive**: Bailar es saludable (dancing is healthy), Nadar es saludable (swimming is healthy), not -ando.
  • **Health/weekday etymology**: salud=health (toast word), saludable=healthy; la luna=moon, weekdays come from planets: lunes/martes/miercoles/jueves/viernes to luna/marte/mercurio/jupiter/venus.

# Lesson 53 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **poder two senses of could**: future could to podria (from poderia dropping e); past could / I used to be able to podia / podiamos (use linear past aba/ia).
  • **Linear past (formation, all persons, main focus this lesson)**: -ar to -aba/-abas/-aba/-abamos/-abais/-aban (buscar to buscaba); -er/-ir to -ia/-ias/-ia/-iamos/-iais/-ian (poder to podia/podiamos, querer to queria, haber to habia).

  • **conmigo not *con mi**: Queria cenar conmigo (he wanted to have dinner with me).

# Lesson 54 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **haber always takes -ado/-ido participle (any form of haber)**: he / habia / habria / habre / infinitive haber to lo habre perdido (I will have lost it), Me gustaria haber ido (I would have liked to go).
  • **haber tense overview (formation)**: present he/has/ha/hemos/habeis/han; linear past habia/habias/habia/habiamos/habiais/habian; conditional habria...; future habre/habras/habra/habremos/habreis/habran; infinitive haber. Participle part never changes.
  • **-ado/-ido participle can be adjective, usually with estar (state, few exceptions)**: esta cerrado, esta terminado, esta perdido, están vendidos.
  • **Participle as adjective needs gender/number agreement (but NEVER when right after haber)**: lo he cerrado / la he cerrado / lo hemos cerrado (always cerrado); as adjective then la puerta cerrada, la pelicula terminada, están vendidos/vendidas, estoy perdido/perdida.

# Lesson 57 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **-er/-ir present differ ONLY in the we-form (formation)**: -er to -emos (comemos), -ir to -imos (vivimos); other persons share endings: come/vive (he), comen/viven (they/you-pl), hablamos (-ar we-form -amos). This is the only place -er and -ir split in all of Spanish.

  • **lo + adjective = "the - thing"**: lo interesante (the interesting thing), lo facil (the easy thing), lo bueno (the good thing), lo malo (the bad thing), lo complicado (the complicated thing): Lo interesante es que viven aqui.
  • **junto(s/a/as)=together, needs gender/number agreement**: viven juntos (masc/mixed), viven juntas (all fem); cognate with junction/join (j is the clue).
  • **facil=easy** (to facilitate = to make easy); **malo=bad** (to malnutrition's mal-).
  • **asi=this way / that way** (most common, replaces de esta forma / de esta manera): No hablamos asi, No comemos asi, No vivimos asi.

# Lesson 58 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Punctual past (preterite) -ar formation**: I to -e (hablar to hable, comprar to compre, enviar to envie), he/she/it/you to -o (hablo, compro, envio), stress on ending vowel.
  • **Stress distinguishes meaning (key)**: HAblo=I speak (present) to habLO=he spoke (punctual past); negative note is no hable (I didn't speak) not no hablo (he didn't speak).
  • **Punctual past vs linear past**: punctual = "a point" (English -ed / ran / ate), linear = "a line" (used to / was -ing); choose by context, not English tense correspondence.
  • **Pronoun position (punctual past)**: lo compre (I bought it), lo compro (he bought it), me lo compro (he bought it for me, no para needed), te lo envie (I sent it to you); add para mi only for emphasis: Lo compro para mi.
  • **todavia=still / yet**: No te lo envie todavia (I haven't sent it to you yet); built from toda+via ("all the way"), splitting helps memory.
  • **se + punctual past = passive/impersonal**: se envio ayer (it was sent yesterday); this structure no longer needs lo (envio already contains "it").
  • **olvidar=to forget**: olvide / olvido; el olvido=forgetfulness (noun, the experience/act of forgetting, common in tango lyrics, no exact English word).

# Lesson 59 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Punctual past -er/-ir formation**: I to -i (comer to comi, salir to sali, ver to vi), he/she/it/you to -io (comio, salio, vio), stress on ending; memorize with hable/hablo (-ar) and comi/comio (-er/-ir).
  • **ver in punctual past is regular**: vi / vio (follows comer): lo vi (I saw him), no me vio (he didn't see me).
  • **que three senses**: what (Que?), that (espero que...), than (comparison): comi mas que el (I ate more than him), comio mas que yo (he ate more than me) — after comparison use nominative el/yo, not me (English "than me" is negative transfer).
  • **Add subject pronouns in contrast**: yo lo vi, pero el no me vio.
  • **Double negation (Spanish requires double negative)**: nada=nothing, nadie=nobody; No vi nada (I saw nothing), No comi nada, Por que no comio nada?, Nadie vio nada (nobody saw anything).

  • **decir (review T39) + attached pronoun**: decirle = tell him/her/you-formal. e.g. I want to tell him to leave me a message to quiero decirle que me deje un mensaje.
  • **cuando + future to subjunctive (review)**: when he leaves you the message, call me to cuando te deje el mensaje, llamame (llamame is llamar's affirmative informal command + me).
  • **-tial / -cial to -cial**: special to especial (review T31); essential to esencial. e.g. it's essential that he sees my message to es esencial que vea mi mensaje (vea based on veo).
  • **ojala + subjunctive = hopefully / I hope**: from Arabic Allah (one theory "ojo-Allah"; ojo = eye / careful, review T65). e.g. hopefully she leaves a message to ojala deje un mensaje; hopefully it'll be soon to ojala sea pronto (sea = ser subjunctive, pronto review).

# Lesson 80 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **se three uses overview (first two are review)**: (1) reflexive/self se: he stays to se queda; (2) double-l clash le/les to se: I prepare it for them to se lo preparo (can't say les lo); (3) unexpected "happens to..." se: se + me/te/le/les/nos.
  • **saber to se / lo se (review)**: I know to yo se; I know it to lo se (se too short, speech often adds lo).
  • **"unexpected se" full persons (folded)**: the door closes (by itself) to la puerta se cierra; the door closes on me to la puerta se me cierra / se me cierra la puerta; the dinner gets burned to la cena se quema; on me to la cena se me quema; the battery runs out to la bateria se acaba; on me/you-informal/you-formal/you-plural to se me acaba / se te acaba / se le acaba / se les acaba (use le for "person", not lo). Word order flexible, subject can go before or after.
  • **Unexpected se in point past (folded)**: it burned to quemo; the dinner got burned to la cena se quemo; on me to se me quemo; on us to se nos quemo.
  • **acabar = to run out / finish**; bateria = battery. e.g. my battery always runs out on me to la bateria siempre se me acaba (siempre review T18; Spanish seldom uses possessive mi, uses definite article).
  • **Other words this lesson are review**: quemar (T47), cenar / cena (T47), puerta / cerrar (T54/T17), preparar (T4), quedar (T25).

# Lesson 81 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Imperfect subjunctive formation**: derive by changing o to a in the last syllable of the preterite "they" (ellos) form. -ar: hablaron to hablaran/hablaras/hablaramos; organizaron to organizaran/organizara/organizamos; llamaron to llamara (he called - subjunctive). -er/-ir: comieron to comieran/comiera/comieras/comieramos.
  • **Main clause in past to que uses imperfect subjunctive**: queria que comieran con nosotros (I wanted them to eat with us); esperaba que me llamara (I had hoped he'd call me).
  • **volver a + infinitive = again (once more)**: vuelve a cerrarse (it closes again); volvi a llamarte (I called you again).
  • **no quiero que + subjunctive (present subjunctive)**: No quiero que la puerta se me cierre.
  • **se me cierre structure**: reflexive/dative pronoun + subjunctive, expresses "the door closes on me".
  • **para + infinitive avoids subjunctive**: volvi a llamarte para organizar (I called you to organize - no subjunctive needed); para que + subjunctive is mandatory (para que organizaramos).
  • **to happen three words**: pasar / ocurrir / suceder.
  • **puerta from puerto (port)**: o to a gives "door".
  • **Imperfect indicative**: expresses past state/habit, -ar/-er/-ir stem + -aba/-ia/-ia (hablaba/comia/vivia); this lesson shows queria (querer I wanted), esperaba (esperar I hoped), necesitabamos (necesitar we needed).
  • **Infinitive + pronoun attachment (infinitivo + clitico)**: known pronouns te/me/lo... attach directly after infinitive, llamarte = llamar + te (call you).

# Lesson 82 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **ser imperfect (line in past) irregular**: era = I was / he was / you-formal were (remember via English "era" epoch); eramos (we), eran (they), eras (you).
  • **lo + adjective = the ... thing (neuter general)**: lo importante (the important thing), lo increible (the unbelievable thing), lo espectacular (the spectacular thing); after it es que ... needs no subjunctive (a structure that avoids subjunctive).
  • **es importante que / es increible que + present subjunctive**: Es increible que vuelvan. (it's unbelievable they're coming back)
  • **creer to creible to increible**: incredible in Spanish is increible (= unbelievable, no d); creer (to believe) to creible (believable) to increible (unbelievable / incredible).
  • **"fact / possible that" can use present or past subjunctive**: No es muy relevante que lo encuentren (present subjunctive) / que lo encontraran (past subjunctive - refers back to past).
  • **volver a + hacerlo = do it again**: Lo increible es que vuelven a hacerlo.

# Lesson 83 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **que + subjunctive = the fact that ... may have ... (expresses "possibility")**: Que lo compraras no es mi problema.
  • **si + imperfect subjunctive = hypothetical past (if I were you / if I could)**: Si pudiera cocinar, cocinaria. (if I could cook, I would cook)
  • **Conditional (-ria) = would**: from "I would like to me gustaria" remember keyword -ria; add -amos/-n/-s. cocinaria, cancelaria, comprenderia, entenderia, gustaria.
  • **poder preterite pud-**: pudieron (they could - preterite); subjunctive pudiera (I could - imperfect subjunctive).
  • **haber preterite hub- + conditional habria**: hubieron (they had - preterite); habria/habria ido (I would have gone - conditional + participle ido); pluperfect subjunctive si me lo hubieras contado (if you had told me).
  • **habria + participle = would have**: habria ido, habriamos ido.
  • **Possessive adjectives mi / tu / su**: mi casa (my house), tu casa (your house), su casa (his/her/your-formal/their house).
  • **Understanding verbs comprender / entender**: both mean understand; conditional comprenderia / entenderia.
  • **Imperfect subjunctive -ase/-iese variants (dialect)**: hablase (=hablara), entendiese (=entendiera), comiese (=comiera), comiesamos (=comieramos) - just recognize, not mandatory.

# Lesson 84 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **-go verbs**: I form uses -go instead of -o: tengo, vengo, salgo, pongo, digo, hago, oigo, traigo. Other persons normal (tiene/tienes/tienen/tenemos).
  • **-go to -ga gives present subjunctive (all persons)**: tenga/tengas, venga/vengas, traiga/traigas, diga.
  • **traer family = tract (drag / bring)**: extraer (extract), atraer (attract), contraer (contract), distraer (distract); I form traigo / distraigo (I distract / distract me).
  • **diga / dime (imperative, phone-answering)**: diga = tell (you-formal), digame = tell me (you-formal); dime = tell me (you).
  • **vale (Spain colloquial)**: = OK / alright / come on (common in Spain).
  • **quiero que + subjunctive (present)**: Quiero que vengas (I want you to come) / que lo traigas (that you bring it) / que me distraigas (don't distract me - negative No quiero que me distraigas).

# Lesson 85 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **-go verbs affirmative imperative (formal = -ga subjunctive; informal = drop -go)**: salga/salgan (you-formal / you-plural leave), ponga/pongalo (you-formal put - lo attaches), ten/tengalo (you-formal have - inf. tenlo), ven/venga (you-formal come - inf. ven), sal (you leave), dime/digame (you / you-formal tell - phone).
  • **Affirmative imperative pronoun position**: lo attaches after verb - pongalo (put it), tenlo (have it), encuentralo (find it - regular verb drops s).
  • **cuando + subjunctive (future)**: Dime cuando vengas (tell me when you come) / Digame cuando lo haga (tell me when you do it - haga = hacer subjunctive).
  • **Regular verb affirmative imperative shortcut**: come (eat), encuentralo (find it) - drop -s, no subjunctive needed.

# Lesson 86 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Future (-re/-ras/-ra/-remos/-ran) and conditional (-ria...) suffixes**: will suffix follows "to-form" + -e/-as/-a/-emos/-an; would suffix follows -ia + -amos/-n/-s.
  • **-go verbs future/conditional contract -go to -dr-**: tendre/tendria/tendriamos (have), vendre/vendria/vendriamos (come), pondre/pondia/pondriamos/pondremos (put), saldria/saldrias/saldras (leave - salir more special), podria/podriamos (can - poder not -go but e drops).
  • **hacer / decir extreme irregular future-conditional**: hare/haremos/hariamos/haran (do), dire/diria/te dire (say / tell).
  • **traer family doesn't contract**: traere / atraere / distraere (future).
  • **haber + past participle = hecho / dicho** (irregular already learned).
  • **mas tarde / esta noche = future context can use present only**: Sales esta noche? (are you going out tonight) replaces Saldras?; Te digo mas tarde (I'll tell you later) replaces Te dire.

# Lesson 87 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **ser imperfect (line in past) full forms**: era/eramos/eran/eras (I was / we were / they were / you were).
  • **ir imperfect special mix**: iba/ibamos/ibas/iban (I went / we went / you went / they went).
  • **ver imperfect**: veia/veiamos/veias/veian (I saw...).
  • **Preterite regular suffixes review**: -ar: hable/hablo/hablamos/hablaron/hablaste; -er/-ir: comi/comio/comimos/comieron/comiste.
  • **tener preterite tuv-**: tuve/tuvo/tuvimos/tuviste/tuvieron (I had / he had / ...).
  • **estar preterite estuv-**: estuve/no estuve/estuvo/estuvimos/estuviste/estuvieron; Donde estuviste?/Donde estuvieron?
  • **tuve que ir = I had to go** (preterite); can use regular tenia que ir to avoid irregular.
  • **decir preterite dij-**: dije/dijo/dijimos (I said / he said / we said).

# Lesson 88 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **poner preterite pus-**: puse/pusiste/donde lo pusiste? (I put / you put / where did you put it); poner contains pos corresponding to compose/expose/oppose.
  • **hacer preterite hic-**: hice/hizo/hicieron (I did / he did / they did).
  • **venir preterite vin-**: vine/vino (I came / he came); vino also = wine.
  • **traer preterite traj-**: traje (I brought / also = suit) / trajo / trajimos (he brought / we brought); Traje un traje (I brought a suit).
  • **fui/fue = ser and ir share preterite**: fui (I was / I went), fue (he was / he went), fuimos (we went), fuiste (you went), fueron (they went / they were); no fui/no fue.
  • **fuer- imperfect subjunctive (ser/ir share)**: fuera (I was / I went - subjunctive), fueran (they were / they went - subjunctive); si fuera tu (if I were you).
  • **tu / usted = you (informal / formal)**: si yo fuera tu / si yo fuera usted.
  • **dijeron/dijera, trajeron, tuvieron to imperfect subjunctive with i-drop**: dijeron to dijera, trajeron to trajera, fueron to fuera (for pronunciation efficiency i drops).
  • **hacer imperfect subjunctive hic-**: queria que lo hicieran / hiciéramos (I wanted them / us to do).

# Lesson 89 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **demasiado (too / excessively)**: split de mas (more) + -iado; can stand alone tengo cuatro de mas (I have four extra). Agrees with noun in gender, stays unchanged with adjective: demasiadas casas / demasiados coches (noun fem/masc pl); demasiado agresivo/agresiva/agresivos/agresivas (adj unchanged).
  • **Feminine words starting with a and bisyllabic use el**: el ala (wing), el agua (water), el aula (classroom) - because la+a is awkward; el agua fria shows it's feminine.
  • **uno/alguno/ninguno drop o before masculine noun**: uno to un, alguno to alguun, ninguno to ningun (un hombre, algun coche, ningun coche); feminine unchanged alguna/ninguna mujer.
  • **uno = one / a, alguno = some, ninguno = none**.

# Lesson 90 — Key Points & What It Covers

  • **Spain dialect le/les for people**: most of Spain uses le/les instead of lo/la/los/las for "him/her/you-formal/them" (person not thing); le veo = I see him/her/you-formal, lo veo = I see it.
  • **vosotros = you-plural (informal, Spain only)**: not used in Latin America.
  • **vos (Rio de la Plata etc ~30% of Spanish area) = you (alongside tu)**: conjugation stress moves to end, puedes to podes, quieres to queres.
  • **Final-lesson gift**: Ahora somos hispanohablantes. (now we are Spanish-speakers)
  • **Pronunciation variants (not words)**: aspirated s: /ehta/ = esta, /ehpero/ = espero.
