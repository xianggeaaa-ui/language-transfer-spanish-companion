# Get the audio (free, official)

The subtitles in this pack are time-aligned to the **Language Transfer Complete Spanish** audio course. The audio itself is **free** and published by Language Transfer — we do **not** redistribute it. Download it once from the official source and pair it with your `.srt` files.

## One-click full download (recommended)
> **https://downloads.languagetransfer.org/spanish/spanish.zip**
>
> A single zip with all 90 lessons of LT Spanish audio. Unzip, then drop your `Lesson NN.srt` next to the matching audio file (filenames are aligned by lesson number).

## Alternative official sources
- **Language Transfer website** — https://www.languagetransfer.org (free, all courses)
- **YouTube** — Language Transfer channel → Complete Spanish playlist
- **SoundCloud / Podcasts** — search "Language Transfer Complete Spanish" (Spotify, Apple Podcasts, etc.)
- **Language Transfer App** (iOS / Android) — free official app

## How to pair
1. Download `spanish.zip` from the link above and unzip.
2. Place your `Lesson 01.srt` (and any booster cards) in the same folder as the lesson-1 audio.
3. Open the audio in any player that supports subtitles (VLC, PotPlayer, Elmedia, etc.) and load the `.srt` — they sync automatically.
